SIMPLE COMPETITIVE CIRCUIT

How Frontline Arena Works

From registration to match completion, the process is streamlined and automated.

01

Register Team

Choose your clan tag, upload a custom emblem avatar, assign your captain gamertag and Discord username.

02

Match Finder

Browse open challenges or post your own match settings with host location and start time directly on the challenge board.

03

Private CDL Veto

Both teams enter the private match room. 3D coin toss decides advantage, followed by turn-by-turn map striking and chat.

04

Report & Dispute Protection

Report scores. If conflicting scores are submitted, our engine automatically files a dispute to admin referees with evidence.
