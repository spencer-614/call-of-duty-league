RULESET OVERVIEW

MW4 6v6 Best-of-5 Order

All 8s series are contested to first-to-3 map victories following the official MW4 6v6 rotation:

  • Map 1: Hardpoint (250 Score Limit, 5 Min Limit)
  • Map 2: Search & Destroy (Round Limit 6, Round Timer 1:30)
  • Map 3: Hijack (300 Score Limit, Data Spike Objective & Comm Station Upload)
  • Map 4: Hardpoint (If needed)
  • Map 5: Search & Destroy (Final decider)
