RULESET OVERVIEW

CDL 2027 4v4 Best-of-3 Order

All 8s series are contested to first-to-2 map victories following the official CDL 2027 (MW4) 4v4 rotation:

  • Map 1 — Hardpoint: 250 Score Limit · 5 Min Limit · 60s Zone · 2.5s Respawn · Killcam Off
  • Map 2 — Search & Destroy: 6 Round Win Limit · 1:30 Round Timer · 1 Life · 5s Plant / 7.5s Defuse
  • Map 3 — Hijack: 150 Score Limit · 4 Min Limit · Unlimited Lives · 4s Respawn · Killcam On (Decider)
Hardpoint (6 Maps): Lotus, Lithium, Munition, Reactor U92, Rooftops, Sentry
Search & Destroy (6 Maps): Lotus, Coal, Munition, Reactor U92, Rooftops, Silkworm
Hijack (3 Maps): Lotus, Lithium, Munition
HOSTING & CDL SETTINGS INTEGRITY

Dedicated Host & Lobby Standards

The designated host is chosen by optimal geographical location (Central / Dallas 60Hz preferred) with strict CDL 2027 Competitive Settings enforced:

  • Friendly Fire: ON · Callout Pings: OFF · Input Swap: OFF
  • Weapon Fire on Minimap: ON · Minimap Radar Always On: OFF
  • LMGs, Marksman Rifles, Shotguns, Launchers, Astaroth .57 & Signal 50: RESTRICTED
  • Mid-game crash before 30s / pre-combat: Immediate remake.
