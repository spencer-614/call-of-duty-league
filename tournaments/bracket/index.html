How Frontline Tournament Brackets Work

1. Automated Seeding & BYE Progression Squads are seeded according to official check-in order and regional standing. In uneven brackets, top seeds receive automatic round 1 BYEs advancing directly to round 2.
2. Map Vetoes & Host Rotation Higher-seeded squad elects Map 1 host on dedicated Central servers (Dallas, TX / Chicago, IL). Standard veto order: HP veto, SnD veto, Control pick, HP pick, SnD remaining.
3. Real-Time Score Verification Referees verify match screenshots and stream timestamps in Discord. The bracket updates automatically and moves winners forward into their next scheduled matchup.
4. Instant Prize Payouts Cash pools are disbursed to winning captains immediately after Grand Finals conclusion via PayPal, CashApp, or Venmo.
