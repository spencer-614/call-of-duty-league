COMPETITIVE RULESET

Frontline CDL Variant Rules

All Frontline Arena Ladder matches are played under official Call of Duty League rules.

CDL Map Rotation (Best of 5)

  • Game 1: Hardpoint (Sub Base, Karachi, Invasion, Skidrow)
  • Game 2: Search & Destroy (Karachi, Highrise, Terminal, Rio)
  • Game 3: Control (Karachi, Invasion, Highrise)
  • Game 4: Hardpoint (Decider map)
  • Game 5: Search & Destroy (Decider map)

Hosting & 15-Minute Rule

  • Coin Flip / High Seed: Choice of first map veto and hosts Games 1, 3, 5.
  • Opponent Host: Hosts Games 2, 4.
  • 15-Minute Grace Period: Teams have exactly 15 minutes past scheduled start time to join lobby. A forfeit dispute may be submitted if opponent fails to appear.
  • Dedicated Server: Central / Dallas server preferred for crossplay equity.

Banned Equipment & GAs

  • Shotguns, Launchers, LMGs, Riot Shields are strictly prohibited.
  • All Battle Rifles and Marksman Rifles banned.
  • Thermobaric Grenades, Breacher Drones, Claymores, Prox Mines banned.
  • Killstreaks: All streaks except Cruise Missile / Predator Missile are banned.

Score Reporting & Disputes

  • Both captains must report the series outcome immediately after completion.
  • Automatic Dispute: If scores conflict, match is locked and transmitted to Admin Console.
  • Proof Required: In any dispute, video clip (Twitch/YouTube/Medal) or scoreboard screenshot must be submitted.
