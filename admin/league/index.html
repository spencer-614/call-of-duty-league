1. Create season

Two preseason weeks, six regular-season weeks. Preseason results do not count toward playoff seeding.

2. Teams and Discord setup

Default: one match per team per week, within each division. Odd divisions receive byes. Repeats begin after the division’s opponent rotation is exhausted. The regular-season rotation starts fresh after preseason.

Week 1–2: preseason. Week 3–8: regular season. Week 9 onward: playoffs.

3. GM assignments

An assigned GM also needs the Discord GM role. Multiple teams per GM are supported.

    4. Playoffs and archiving

    Default: all enrolled teams qualify for single elimination within their division. Seed by regular-season wins, losses, then map difference. Resolve remaining ties before generating. Higher seeds receive byes. Winners advance automatically when staff finalize results on the website.

    Finalize or cancel all matches first. Archiving makes season channels read-only and closes voice access. It preserves match records and roster history.
